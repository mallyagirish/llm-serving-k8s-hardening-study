# External label and methodology review: provenance record

**Status:** Author-reported review; supporting reviewer documentation pending (2026-11-12).

## Confirmed by the study author

- A second person reviewed **all 2,940 bundle–rule labels** (105 primary bundles × 28 rules).
- The second person also reviewed the **labeling methodology**.
- The second person validated existing labels; the author has **not** described a blind, independently generated second annotation matrix.
- This is a **full-matrix external review of existing labels and methodology**, not evidence of independent double annotation.

## Details not yet established

The following information is required before a detailed peer-reviewable audit claim can be made:

1. Reviewer's expertise, independence from the initial labeling process, and any relevant conflicts of interest (describe without publishing private identifying details without permission).
2. Review date(s), label-file version and SHA-256, and labeling-guide version inspected.
3. Whether the reviewer examined source YAML, evidence excerpts, author rationales, and/or initial AI labels; which of these were visible.
4. Whether the reviewer recorded decisions per row or simply approved the matrix after inspection.
5. Number and identity of challenged labels, if any, plus resolution history. **Do not assume zero disagreements.**
6. Whether any label changes were made after this review and, if so, whether the reviewer inspected the final frozen v2 file.
7. A dated review statement or audit log from the reviewer, if available.

## Reporting boundary

Acceptable provisional wording:

> Following author validation, a second person reviewed all 2,940 existing bundle–rule labels and the labeling methodology. The review was an external assessment of the existing reference labels, rather than an independently generated blind annotation set. Detailed review procedures and disagreement records are not yet available; no inter-rater reliability or reviewer-agreement statistic is claimed.

**Not supported:** “two independent human annotators,” “100% agreement,” “Cohen's κ,” “blind validation,” “independent ground truth,” or “no disagreements.”

## Evidence-to-claim gate

Do not upgrade this claim until reviewer-generated or contemporaneous documentation supports it. A retrospective author statement can establish the author's report, but cannot substitute for a recorded audit trail. Preserve original review evidence, and document any later adjudications without retroactively rewriting frozen labels.
